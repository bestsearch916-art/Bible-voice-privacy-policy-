Bible Voice Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

Bible Voice respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Bible Voice mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Bible Voice is designed primarily for Bible reading, searching and voice-based Bible passage discovery. We do not require you to create an account to use the main Bible reading and search features.

When you use voice search, the application may request access to your device microphone so that your spoken command can be processed for Bible search purposes.

2. Microphone and Voice Search

Bible Voice uses microphone access only when you choose to use the voice-search feature. Your microphone is not intended to be accessed continuously in the background.

Voice recognition may use services provided by the device operating system or other speech-recognition technology. The handling of voice data by such services is subject to the privacy policies of the relevant service providers.

3. Bible Content and Search

Bible Voice provides Bible passages and search functionality. Search queries may be processed locally within the application where supported.

4. Advertising

Bible Voice uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner and interstitial advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and use certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address and advertising-related information, in accordance with Google's applicable policies and privacy practices.

You can learn more about how Google handles information through Google's privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Third-Party Services

Bible Voice may use third-party services that help provide advertising, speech recognition, application functionality and analytics or other technical services.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's Privacy

Bible Voice is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Bible Voice does not require users to create an account for its core Bible reading and search functionality. Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the policies of those services.

9. Your Choices

You may control permissions granted to Bible Voice through your device settings. For example, you can deny or revoke microphone permission. If microphone permission is denied, voice-search functionality may not work.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Bible Voice's privacy practices, you may contact the application developer through the contact information provided with the application.

12. Acceptance

By using Bible Voice, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
